GolfTracker Support

GolfTracker is an iPhone app for quick on-course shot logging, miss tracking, and post-round review.

Support

For support, questions, bug reports, or feature requests, use the public support issue tracker:

GolfTrackerSupport Issues

Common Help

  • Tap a shot type to start logging a shot.
  • Select the fault that best describes the miss.
  • Use the summary and chart views after a round to review patterns.
  • Use Clear All Data in the summary view to remove stored shot logs from the device.

Privacy Policy

GolfTracker stores shot logs locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not transmit shot data to a server.

Data Collection

GolfTracker does not collect personal data, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track users across apps or websites.

Local Storage

Shot entries are stored on the device so the app can show summaries and charts. You can delete stored entries from within the app.

Contact
